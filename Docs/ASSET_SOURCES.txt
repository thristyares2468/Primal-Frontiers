PRIMAL FRONTIER - ASSET SHORTLIST
=================================

Compiled 2026-10-05. Assets picked for a realistic first-person prehistoric-fantasy survival game:
temperate starting forest, wildlife and dinosaurs, primitive tools, tiered base building, ancient
sites and dimensional "Frontier" biomes (volcanic, frozen, crystal, overgrown, corrupted).

Legend
  [FREE]         Listed as free when searched (Epic free/permanent collection or creator-free).
  [FREE-ENDED]   Was a limited-time free item. Free only if you already claimed it.
  (no tag)       Paid or price unknown. Check the listing.

Before buying anything
  - These links come from web searches on 2026-10-05. Fab could not be opened directly from the
    machine that compiled this list, so confirm price, "Supported Engine Versions" (UE 5.8) and
    license on each listing before buying.
  - Old unrealengine.com/marketplace links redirect to the matching Fab listing.
  - Fab Standard License: commercial use, any engine. Epic samples (Electric Dreams, Valley of
    the Ancient, Project Titan, Game Animation Sample) are Unreal-Engine-only.
  - Several packs include their own Blueprint gameplay (building, crafting, weather). Use their
    meshes, materials, animations and sounds. Gameplay authority stays in the project's C++.
  - Keep the roster original: recolor, rename and kit-bash creature and character packs. Never
    import anything from ARK, Palworld or other games (see References/SurvivalGames/README.md).
  - Store imports under Content/PrimalFrontier (AGENTS.md).

----------
Everything above is realistic or semi-realistic, so it should sit together under Lumen and
Nanite. Avoid mixing in hand-painted or low-poly stylized packs unless the art direction changes.
The one stylized exception above (Crystal Cave) only needs material tuning.
