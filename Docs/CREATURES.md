\# Creatures



\## Creature Principles



\- Every creature needs a readable gameplay role.

\- Creature behaviour should be understandable before it becomes complex.

\- Creatures should support survival, exploration, combat, or settlement gameplay.

\- Creature companionship should feel earned and useful.



\## Initial Creature Types



\### Passive Creature



\- Wanders and feeds.

\- Avoids players or threats.

\- Provides basic resources.

\- Creates life and ambience in the world.



\### Hostile Creature



\- Patrols a territory.

\- Detects and pursues players.

\- Attacks with clear tells.

\- Provides useful but riskier resources.



\## Creature Companionship



The early prototype should test:



\- Finding a compatible creature

\- Building trust or completing a simple taming process

\- Ownership

\- Following

\- Staying

\- Basic commands

\- Creature health

\- Creature persistence



\## Future Creature Roles



\- Pack animal

\- Scout

\- Resource gatherer

\- Settlement defender

\- Frontier specialist

\- Rare or legendary creature
