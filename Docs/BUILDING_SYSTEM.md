\# Building System



\## Goal



Players should be able to build shelters that grow into functional settlements.



Building must be useful for survival, storage, safety, crafting, creature management, and later industrial progression.



\## Initial Structures



\- Foundation

\- Wall

\- Doorway

\- Door

\- Ceiling or roof

\- Campfire

\- Storage container



\## Placement Rules



\- The player sees a first-person placement preview.

\- Green preview means valid placement.

\- Red preview means invalid placement.

\- The server validates all placed structures.

\- Pieces snap to compatible structures.

\- Structures cannot overlap invalid terrain, blocked objects, or existing structures.

\- Building requires required resources.



\## Ownership



Initial ownership rules:



\- The placer owns the structure.

\- Cooperative players can be granted access.

\- Storage access is permission-controlled.

\- Future group or settlement systems can expand this.



\## Future Building Features



\- More material tiers

\- Stairs

\- Defensive pieces

\- Crafting stations

\- Farming

\- Creature enclosures

\- Power or industrial systems

\- Settlement permissions

\- Repair

\- Demolition

\- Decay rules
