# Core Loop

## Moment-to-Moment Gameplay

Players move through the world, observe threats and opportunities, gather resources, manage survival needs, interact with creatures, and make tactical decisions.

Typical actions include:

- Exploring
- Gathering
- Hunting
- Avoiding danger
- Fighting
- Crafting
- Building
- Taming or befriending creatures
- Transporting resources
- Researching technology
- Preparing for expeditions

## Early-Game Loop

1. Gather basic wood, stone, fibre, food, and water.
2. Craft primitive tools.
3. Establish a small shelter.
4. Survive nearby environmental and creature threats.
5. Expand inventory, crafting, and building capability.

## Mid-Game Loop

1. Build a defensible settlement.
2. Establish storage, crafting stations, and resource routes.
3. Form creature companionships.
4. Explore more dangerous regions.
5. Unlock advanced materials and technology.

## Late-Game Loop

1. Research ancient and dimensional technology.
2. Prepare equipment and creatures for expeditions.
3. Enter unstable Frontiers.
4. Secure rare resources and knowledge.
5. Bring discoveries back to Eryndor.
6. Unlock new Frontiers and master increasingly dangerous environments.

## Session Loop

```text
