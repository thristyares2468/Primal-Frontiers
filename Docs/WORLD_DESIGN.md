\# World Design



\## Eryndor



Eryndor is the primary prehistoric-fantasy world of Primal Frontier.



It is a large world shaped by ancient forces and fractured by unstable dimensional regions called Frontiers.



Eryndor should feel natural, dangerous, ancient, and worth settling.



\## Starting Region



The initial playable region should include:



\- Temperate forest

\- Fresh water source

\- Stone and wood resources

\- Small open building areas

\- Passive wildlife

\- Low-level predators

\- A visible but unreachable dangerous landmark



\## Resource Distribution



\- Common resources should be available near the player start.

\- Valuable resources should require greater risk or preparation.

\- Rare materials should encourage expeditions.

\- Frontier-specific materials should unlock later progression.



\## Frontiers



Frontiers are unstable dimensional regions that contain unusual environments, creatures, hazards, materials, and ancient knowledge.



Each Frontier should have:



\- A distinct visual identity

\- New environmental threats

\- New resources

\- New creature behaviours

\- A clear preparation requirement

\- A meaningful progression reward



\## Example Frontier Types



\- Volcanic frontier

\- Frozen frontier

\- Overgrown primordial frontier

\- Crystal or ancient-technology frontier

\- Storm or sky frontier

\- Corrupted dimensional frontier



These are concepts, not final commitments.
