\# Items and Crafting



\## Technology Tiers



\### Primitive



\- Stone tools

\- Torches

\- Basic weapons

\- Simple clothing

\- Campfires

\- Basic structures



\### Settlement



\- Storage

\- Workbenches

\- Improved tools

\- Better building materials

\- Farming or food preservation

\- Creature equipment



\### Industrial



\- Advanced crafting stations

\- Power or fuel systems

\- Processing equipment

\- Advanced weapons and armour

\- Settlement infrastructure



\### Ancient and Dimensional



\- Ancient research equipment

\- Frontier traversal tools

\- Dimensional protection

\- Rare energy systems

\- High-tier creature equipment



\## Initial Resources



\- Wood

\- Stone

\- Fibre

\- Plant food

\- Water

\- Hide or equivalent

\- Raw meat

\- Metal ore



\## Initial Crafting Items



\- Stone pick

\- Stone axe

\- Torch

\- Campfire

\- Basic storage container

\- Foundation

\- Wall

\- Doorway

\- Door

\- Basic weapon



\## Item Data Requirements



Every item should define:



\- Unique identifier

\- Display name

\- Description

\- Icon

\- Weight

\- Stack limit

\- Item category

\- World representation

\- Crafting recipe, if applicable

\- Technology tier

\- Durability, if applicable
