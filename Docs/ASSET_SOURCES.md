PRIMAL FRONTIER - FREE ASSET LIST
=================================

Compiled 2026-10-06. Every asset below is free. Assets were picked for a realistic first-person
prehistoric-fantasy survival game: temperate starting forest, wildlife and dinosaurs, primitive
tools, base building, ancient sites and dimensional "Frontier" biomes.

Things with no good free option are listed at the end under GAPS. Those are candidates to make
yourself (e.g. with Higgsfield) or to buy later.


LICENSE KEY
-----------
  [CC0]        Public domain. Any use, no credit needed.
  [CC-BY]      Free, but you must credit the author in the game's credits (see CREDITS below).
  [EPIC]       Epic Games free content. Commercial use allowed, Unreal Engine projects only.
  [FAB-FREE]   Free on Fab under the Fab Standard License (commercial use, any engine).
  [UNITY-FREE] Free on the Unity Asset Store. The standard Asset Store EULA allows use in other
               engines, but you need the (free) Unity editor to unpack the .unitypackage.
  [FREE*]      Shown as free when searched, but not confirmed on the listing itself.

Before using anything
  - Fab could not be opened from the machine that compiled this list, so "free" comes from search
    results. Fab and Sketchfab show the price and license on each page. Check before adding.
  - Fab also runs time-limited free giveaways. Those are NOT listed here, except where noted.
  - Sketchfab: only use models with a "Download" button and a CC0 or CC-BY license. Avoid
    "NonCommercial" (NC) and "NoDerivs" (ND) licenses. Avoid models ripped from other games
    (anything named after Jurassic Park/World, Dino Crisis, Turok, Carnivores, ARK, etc.).
  - Quaternius and Kenney packs are low-poly cartoon style. Use them as placeholders only.
  - Some packs ship their own Blueprint gameplay. Use only the meshes, animations, materials and
    sounds; the game's C++ handles gameplay.
  - Store imports under Content/PrimalFrontier (AGENTS.md).

Handy Fab pages (no download, just browsing)
  Epic's free Fab content hub      https://www.unrealengine.com/fabfreecontent
  Epic's free content docs         https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/free-epic-games-content-for-unreal-engine
  On fab.com itself, use the search filter "Price: Free" to find more.

GAPS - NO GOOD FREE ASSET FOUND (make these, e.g. with Higgsfield)
==================================================================
Each line says what kind of asset is needed so it can be made in the right format.

 1. Building pieces, primitive tier - stick/thatch/hide foundation, wall, doorway, door,
    ceiling/roof. Static meshes, modular to the game's 400 cm build grid.
 2. Building pieces, stone and metal tiers - same piece set as above. (Free wood building is
    covered by Modular Rural Cabins; nothing free matches stone or metal.)
 3. Realistic primitive tools and weapons - stone axe/hatchet, club, bow and arrows, torch,
    knife. Static meshes. (Pickaxe and spears have free Sketchfab options above.)
 4. First-person tool animations - chop, mine, spear thrust, bow draw/release, torch hold,
    equip/unequip, eat/drink. Arm animations on the UE5 mannequin skeleton.
 5. Camp and crafting stations - realistic campfire with cooking spit, crafting bench, drying
    rack, kiln/furnace, food preservation bin, sleeping mat/bed. Static meshes. (Only low-poly
    Kenney placeholders are free.)
 6. Survivor clothing - hide, fur and woven-fibre prehistoric outfits for the full-body
    character (MetaHuman-compatible or UE5 mannequin).
 7. More realistic herbivore dinosaurs - triceratops, ankylosaurus, parasaurolophus,
    brachiosaurus, plus feeding/sleeping/death animations. Rigged and animated skeletal meshes.
    (Free realistic options only cover T-rex, raptor, pachycephalosaurus, stegosaurus and a
    sauropod; the rest exist only as low-poly Quaternius models.)
 8. Original Frontier creatures - creature designs unique to Primal Frontier. Rigged + animated.
 9. Crystal Frontier - crystal clusters, crystal-veined rocks, glowing crystal formations.
    Static meshes with emissive materials.
10. Frozen Frontier meshes - ice formations, frozen waterfalls, snow-covered rocks and dead
    trees. (Free snow/ice textures exist; meshes don't.)
11. Volcanic Frontier meshes - lava rock formations, obsidian, vents, scorched trees.
    (Free lava material exists; meshes don't.)
12. Ancient / dimensional technology - Frontier portals and gates, research stations, ancient
    machinery, dimensional artifacts. Static meshes plus emissive materials.
13. Realistic item icons in a consistent painted style - 2D square icons (e.g. 512 px,
    transparent background) for every item, resource and recipe. game-icons.net is flat
    silhouettes only.
14. Tech-tree and HUD art - panel frames, tier-row backgrounds, ordinary vs challenge point
    currency icons, status icons (hunger, thirst, exposure, freshness).
15. Per-species creature voice sets - idle, alert, attack, hurt, death for each creature.
    Free sounds exist only as scattered single roars.
16. Original music themes - main theme, exploration, combat, Frontier ambience. Free packs are
    generic.
